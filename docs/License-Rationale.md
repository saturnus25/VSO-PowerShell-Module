The module uses a custom VSO PowerShell Module Source-Available License 1.0. Free personal and professional use, study and technical copies are permitted. Exact unmodified sharing with complete attribution is permitted; general modification, derivative projects, modified distribution and rebranding are not granted. It must not be described as OSI-approved open source.

Existing licenses considered did not express this boundary: the [Functional Source License](https://fsl.software/) grants broader permissions and later converts to an open-source license; the [Business Source License](https://mariadb.com/bsl11/) permits modifications and has a change date; the [Elastic License 2.0](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license) permits modification and redistribution subject to its own restrictions. None is presented as the module's license.

GitHub's [Terms of Service, section D](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service) grant platform permissions and permit public-repository viewing and forks through GitHub. The custom license expressly preserves these independently granted rights. It cannot promise that public source cannot be copied or forked.

PowerShell Gallery's [Terms of Use](https://www.powershellgallery.com/policies/Terms) recognize package-specific license terms, while requiring permissions to obtain, save, reproduce and share packages. The license allows exact attributed unmodified sharing and preserves platform permissions. The [publishing guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/powershell/gallery/concepts/publishing-guidelines) support a package LicenseUri. The manifest points to the tagged module license, and the package includes LICENSE. Installation does not add a custom acceptance wizard; published terms accompany every copy.

The application and third-party licenses remain independent. No restriction here purports to revoke an earlier grant or override mandatory law. This is a technical comparison against the cited platform terms, not a legal opinion or a claim of legal review. Enforceability across jurisdictions has not been assessed by counsel.
